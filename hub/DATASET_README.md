# EuroPII benchmark

**2,490 synthetic messages in 12 European languages with character-level
annotations of personal data and hard negatives**, for evaluating PII
detection and redaction tools on European identifiers.

Built by [Since AI](https://sinceai.ai) for
[EuroPII Guard](https://github.com/sinceaihq/europii-guard). Everything is
generated by code in that repository (`python -m europii.bench.synthetic
--seed 2026 --dev 5 --test 25`) and can be regenerated byte for byte.

## Content

Messages come from five templates per language — customer support request,
HR record, refund request, login alert, supplier details — and an unlabelled
contact-list row (`name; id; e-mail; phone`). English messages additionally
quote identifiers of 11 further countries (NO, BE, EE, LT, LV, SK, SI, RO,
BG, HR, AT).

| split | messages | seed |
|---|---|---|
| dev | 415 | 2027 |
| test | 2,075 | 2026 |

Languages: English (Ireland), Finnish, Swedish, German, French, Dutch,
Italian, Spanish, Polish, Portuguese, Czech, Danish.

Each line:

```json
{"id": "fi-1-003", "language": "fi", "template": 1,
 "text": "Työntekijä Aino Nieminen, henkilötunnus 270486A0459, palkkatili FI64 5744 1675 1687 21. Työsuhde alkaa 7.7.2025, kuukausipalkka 8 886,55 €.",
 "spans": [{"start": 11, "end": 24, "label": "PERSON"},
           {"start": 40, "end": 51, "label": "NATIONAL_ID", "detector": "fi_hetu"},
           {"start": 64, "end": 86, "label": "IBAN"}],
 "negatives": [{"start": 103, "end": 111, "kind": "DATE"},
               {"start": 128, "end": 138, "kind": "AMOUNT"}]}
```

**PII labels**: `PERSON`, `EMAIL`, `PHONE`, `IBAN`, `PAYMENT_CARD`,
`IP_ADDRESS`, `VAT_ID`, `NATIONAL_ID` (with the identifier type in
`detector`).

**Hard negatives** (never personal data): `ORDER` and `INVOICE` numbers,
`DATE`, `AMOUNT`, `EAN` barcode, `POSTCODE`, `BAD_IBAN` (wrong check digits)
and `BAD_ID` (a national-ID look-alike whose checksum or date fails).

## How the values were made safe

* Identifiers that encode a birth date use dates **no living person can
  have**: future dates (FI, PL, EE, LT, CZ, SK, SI, RO, BG), early-19th-century
  dates before the numbering scheme existed (SE, NO, DK, LV), or 1900–1905
  (BE).
* Identifiers without a date (DE, NL, ES, PT, IE, HR, AT, FR, IT) are random
  numbers that pass the official checksum. They may coincide with an issued
  number, but nothing else in the dataset relates to any real person.
* Payment cards are the networks' public **test card numbers**; e-mail
  addresses use the reserved `example.com/.org/.net` domains (RFC 2606);
  IPv6 addresses use the documentation prefix `2001:db8::/32`.
* Names are combinations of common first and last names. Phone numbers are
  random numbers valid under libphonenumber; any match with a real number is
  coincidental.

## Intended use and limitations

For measuring recall and false positives of PII detectors on European
formats. The messages are template-based and much cleaner than real
documents: good scores here are necessary, not sufficient, for real-world
use. Not suitable for training models meant to generalise to real text
without additional data.
